## 2.0

> **Upgrading a v1.x save:** save inside a Pokémon Center before you switch to 2.0. Some places got new walls (Route 2's old mini forest and parts of Route 17), and a save made standing on one of those spots loads you inside the wall.

### New places
- **Viridian Forest** is back, ported from Yellow, between two new gates on Route 2. It follows the time of day and plays Route 2's old music. Route 2's mini forest is gone; its items moved into the real forest.
- **Pewter Museum** is open, ported from Red/Blue: two floors, a ¥60 ticket desk, and the Aerodactyl and Kabutops fossil exhibits. Pewter's "museum closed" sign is gone.

### New features
- **[Starter Injector](https://joseph-bayer.github.io/crystal-crunch-starter-injector/)**: a tool that injects the starter you want into your patched ROM, in your browser. Pick any of the 251 in any form, normal, shiny or alternate shiny, or make up to 5 Pokémon of your own: a new line, or new members of one of the game's lines. New Pokémon can't go over a link: the Cable Club turns them away.
- **Blackjack** at the Goldenrod and Celadon Game Corners: talk to the dealer from the front of the table, bet 10, 50 or 100 coins, and play with Meowth cheering you on. There's a second deck to find.
- **Nayru's Pokédex**: see where to find a species, its evolution line, and its front, back and overworld pics. Tabs wrap around, you don't wait for animations or cries, and it shows the forms and alternate shinies you've caught (press SELECT to cycle them). Pick the Pokédex's frame color, and toggle shiny pics.
- **Pokégear clock card**: shows the time of day, where you are, and the fishing group.
- **Trainer color**: pick red, blue or green at the start, and buy more colors from the Stylist in the Goldenrod Underground. Her stock changes daily, and switching between colors you own is free.
- **Moped**: a key item from the new Moped Shop in Cerulean City. It goes as fast as the Bicycle, and faster while you hold B. The Bicycle's pedalling no longer runs too fast, and the Moped plays Totodile's theme from Pokémon Puzzle Challenge. Cycling Road has new ledges and moved trainers to suit it.
- **Red/Blue Pokémon play like Gen 1**: Red/Blue forms, and the beta Pichu, Igglybuff, Crobat and Kingdra, have one Special stat and no gender, with new breeding rules (see [spoilers.md](spoilers.md#breeding-redblue-pokémon)).
- **Additional Hidden Power/Unown Updates**:
  - Unown are the type of their Hidden Power, so it gets STAB. The Pokédex lists their type as ???.
  - Hidden Power only grows while an Unown is in your party, and each Pokémon rolls for itself. The GLYPH SHARD gives an extra chance, with an Unown in the party.
  - The Hidden Power stats page also shows the power, Physical or Special, and a star when it's maxed.
  - The Shaman has moved into a cave in the Ruins of Alph.
  - Unown party icons are black, or navy when shiny, so a shiny Unown stands out.

### Trainers and battles
- **Kanto scales with your badges**: the Gym Leaders, gym and route trainers, and your rival at Mt. Moon, so you can take Kanto's gyms in any order.
- **About 40 new trainers** across Kanto's routes and Viridian Forest, scaling with your badges too.
- **Trainers' Return and Frustration** hit at full power, as their Hidden Power does.
- **Missing level-up moves** from Red, Blue and Yellow are back (for example, Snorlax learns Harden and Double-Edge again), and a few repeated moves are gone.
- **Furret** learns Baton Pass at level 50.

### Shinies and swarms
- **Overworld shinies**: wandering Pokémon are shiny at 1/2048 (was 1/512). The last 5 overworld shinies are remembered, swarms and populations included: leave, battle something else, or save and quit, and the shiny is still there until you battle it.
- **The shiny chime** no longer plays for shinies that aren't on the map, and shiny roaming legendaries now chime.
- **More alternate shinies**: the Bulbasaur, Charmander and Squirtle lines, the Weedle line, Eevee and its evolutions, and Articuno.
- **Swarm radio** moves to station 6.0 and picks two swarms a day: one from midnight to 5 PM, and one from 5 PM to midnight. Up to four swarms can run at once.
- **New swarms**: RB Pikachu in Viridian Forest (after the Elite Four), and an alternate-shiny Weedle swarm on Route 30. RB Pikachu no longer appears in the Hidden Grove.
- **Team Rocket's swarm Pokémon** know Thief and Frustration and start with no happiness; a Friend Ball only brings them up to 100. The Rocket hideout's Electrodes start with no happiness too.

### Music
- Songs from the Pokémon Trading Card Game play in Ilex Forest and Apricorn Forest's clearing.
- Totodile's theme from Pokémon Puzzle Challenge play while riding the moped.

### Smaller changes and fixes
- Apricorn Forest's path into the clearing is open again.
- Bill's PC shows an alternate shiny's own icon.
- The Photo Studio's printout shows a Pokémon's real Pokédex number.
- New overworld and party icons for the beta Kingdra, beta Pichu and RB Cloyster, and a new overworld icon for Snorlax's Apricorn form.

### Known with old saves
- Pokémon caught at Pewter City or anywhere after it (Route 3 onward, the Fast Ship, Apricorn Forest) show their caught place as the one before it, for example "Viridian Forest" for Pewter City.
